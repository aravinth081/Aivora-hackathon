Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Official answers based on verified Innovation Hacks and AIVORA 2K26 event rules.

Innovation Hacks is a technology and innovation platform dedicated to empowering students, developers, and creators to transform creative ideas into practical digital solutions through hackathons, software products, and real-world problem-solving opportunities under the motto BUILD • INNOVATE • IMPACT.
AIVORA 2K26 is the premier global online AI hackathon organized by Innovation Hacks and hosted on Unstop. It invites visionary innovators and developers worldwide to architect groundbreaking artificial intelligence solutions across five curated challenge tracks.
The competition was open to students, developers, innovators, researchers, and technology enthusiasts globally, subject to the official eligibility criteria specified on the Unstop listing.
According to the verified official Unstop event listing, AIVORA 2K26 welcomes both individual developers and teams. Each registrant or team builds and submits their own original AI solution.
Five tracks are offered:
  1. AI for Everyday Life
  2. Smart & Sustainable Future
  3. Digital Safety & Cybersecurity
  4. Inclusive Technology
  5. Future of Work & Automation
Participants must submit their project details, accessible GitHub repository URL containing the source code, a video walkthrough demonstration, and a working live demo link if available.
Submissions are judged against five official criteria: Innovation & Originality, AI / Technical Implementation, Problem Relevance & Impact, Functionality, and Presentation & Demo.
Yes. All participants who submit an eligible project in accordance with the official rules receive an authenticated Certificate of Participation. Top distinction winners receive Certificates of Achievement, and evaluators receive Certificates of Appreciation.
According to the verified official Unstop event listing, the top three positions (Global Winner, Global Runner-up, and Global Second Runner-up) are eligible for a one-month online internship opportunity with Innovation Hacks, along with their distinction certificates and featured showcase.
Official announcements are published on the verified Unstop competition portal and cross-communicated through Innovation Hacks official channels.
